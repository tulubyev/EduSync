## 2. What Data We Process

| Category | Data |
|---|---|
| Account | first name, last name, email, phone number (if provided), country, password hash, profile photo (if uploaded), time zone, language |
| Learning | courses, programs, lessons, webinars, deadlines, exams, progress, notes and library materials |
| Connected platforms | the user's identifier on the platform, access keys (stored in encrypted form), course and schedule data received from the platform |
| Webinar recordings | stream link, time, title, webinar login details (if provided), recording files |
| AI assistant | the user's messages and information about their courses and deadlines that is sent to the assistant to generate a response |
| Payment | subscription and payment information; the Operator does not receive or store bank card data — it is processed by the payment service |
| Technical data | IP address, browser and device type, date and time of requests, error logs |

The Operator does not process special categories of personal data (health, beliefs, etc.) or biometric
data.

The Operator collects, records, systematizes, accumulates, stores, updates, retrieves, uses,

| Recipient | Purpose | Where it processes data |

## 6. Where Data Is Stored

6.1. The recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, updating and retrieval of personal data of citizens
of the Russian Federation are carried out using databases located in the territory of the Russian
Federation (Part 5 of Article 18 of Law No. 152-FZ).

6.2. The Operator plans separate storage environments for users from other countries. Until they are launched, the data
of all users is stored on servers in Russia; users from other countries give separate consent to this
upon registration (see Sections 7 and 10).

## 7. Cross-Border Transfer

7.1. Cross-border transfer may occur in two cases: when the AI assistant is used (the servers of OpenRouter
and the model providers are located in the USA and other countries), and when the user connects a platform that
is located outside Russia.

7.2. The Operator notifies Roskomnadzor (the Russian data protection authority) of cross-border transfer in accordance with Article 12 of Law No. 152-FZ. Only the user's
messages and information about their courses and deadlines are sent to the AI assistant — without email, phone number or passwords.

7.3. The user may choose not to use the AI assistant and not to connect foreign platforms; all other
functions of the Service remain available.

## 8. Retention Periods

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account and learning data | for as long as the account exists; deleted within 30 days after the account is deleted |
| Platform access keys | until the user disconnects the platform or deletes the account |
| Webinar recordings | according to the Help Recorder plan; deleted together with the account |

| Technical logs | up to 30 days |

## 9. User Rights

The user has the right to:
- obtain information about the processing of their data and a copy of it;
- demand that the data be updated, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated or
  processed unlawfully;
- withdraw consent to processing and delete their account — by email to support@myepa.ru; after withdrawal, the Operator
  stops processing and deletes the data within 30 days, except for data it is required by law to retain;
- disconnect any connected platform;
- appeal against the Operator's actions to Roskomnadzor or in court.

The Operator responds to a request within 10 working days (this period may be extended by 5 working days
with notice).

## 10. Users from the EU and EEA (GDPR)

10.1. Legal bases for processing: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — for operating the account and calendar;
consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for connecting platforms, reminders, the AI assistant and webinar recording;
legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — for the security of the Service.

10.2. Data is stored in Russia. There is no European Commission adequacy decision for Russia, so the
transfer is based on the user's explicit consent (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR), which the user gives upon registration
after being informed of the risks: Russia may not ensure a level of protection equivalent to the
European level, and the user may not have the same means of protecting their rights.

10.3. Additional rights: the right to data portability, the right to object to processing based on legitimate
interest, and the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of their country.

10.4. Representative in the EU (Art. 27 GDPR): not appointed.

## 11. Children

11.1. Users under the age of 14 may register only with the consent of a parent or other legal representative
(the "Legal Representative Consent" document).

11.2. For users from the EU, the age at which a child may give consent on their own is determined by the law of their
country (from 13 to 16 years, Art. 8 GDPR); below that age, parental consent is required.

## 12. Data Protection

The Operator applies legal, organizational and technical measures: encryption of connections (HTTPS), storage of
passwords only as hashes, encryption of platform access keys, restriction of staff access,
limiting the number of login attempts, daily encrypted backups of the database. The Operator notifies Roskomnadzor and users of security breaches affecting personal
data within the time limits established by law.

## 13. Cookies

The Service uses only strictly necessary cookies and the browser's local storage — see the "Cookie Policy"
(https://myepa.ru/cookies).
