5.2. The user requests recording only of events they are entitled to record (as a participant in the event, with

5.3. A recording is intended solely for the user's personal use. Its distribution is prohibited unless

5.4. Recording is performed by the Help Recorder service. The retention period and maximum recording length depend on the plan.
