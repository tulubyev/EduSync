# Data Exchange Agreement (Template) — My EPA and a Learning Platform

Version of [DATE]. Parties: [OPERATOR NAME] (Tax ID (INN) [TAX ID (INN)]) — "My EPA" — and [PLATFORM NAME]
([COMPANY DETAILS]) — the "Platform".

## 1. Subject Matter

1.1. The Platform provides My EPA with data on programs, courses, lessons, webinars, assignments, deadlines, exams,
links to materials and (where agreed) the progress of students who have connected the Platform in My EPA.

1.2. My EPA displays this data to the student in a calendar, reminds them of deadlines and directs the student via links to
the Platform.

1.3. The exchange is free of charge for both parties.

## 2. Exchange Method

The parties choose one or more methods (tick as applicable):
- [ ] a Platform export in the My EPA format, which My EPA retrieves on a schedule;
- [ ] the Platform sending changes to the My EPA partner API;
- [ ] the Platform's API, to which My EPA connects;
- [ ] the student's iCal calendar.

Technical description: the My EPA format specification (version [VERSION]) and/or the Platform's API documentation.
Access is via keys that the parties issue to each other and keep secret.

## 3. Students and Consent

3.1. A particular student's data is transferred only after the student has connected the Platform in My EPA and given
consent to the transfer (login via the Platform, confirmation code, email or personal link).

3.2. The student may disconnect the Platform at any time; after that, My EPA stops receiving their data and
deletes the data received from the Platform within 30 days.

## 4. Roles in the Processing of Personal Data

4.1. With respect to the data that the student receives in My EPA, each party is an independent operator (controller): the Platform —
for its own data about the student; My EPA — for the data it processes for the student in its service.

4.2. Each party is itself responsible for having legal grounds for processing, notifying the regulator and responding to data
subject requests for its own part.

4.3. If, for a student from the EU, one party processes data on behalf of the other, the parties additionally
sign a Data Processing Agreement (Section 8) and, where necessary, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. Scope of Data

Only the data needed for the purposes of Section 1 is transferred: the student's name or identifier on the Platform, email for
matching (if this method is chosen), courses and schedule, links, progress (if agreed). Passwords,
payment data and special categories of data are not transferred.

## 6. Security

The parties use encryption in transit (HTTPS), keep access keys secret, restrict staff
access and notify each other of incidents affecting students' data no later than 72 hours after
discovery.

## 7. Use of Trademarks

My EPA may display the Platform's name and logo in the catalogue and in the student's calendar. The Platform may state
that its courses are available in My EPA.

## 8. Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — Annex, If Applicable

8.1. Subject matter and duration: processing on behalf of the other party for the term of the Agreement.
8.2. Nature and purpose: [DESCRIBE].
8.3. Categories of data and data subjects: students who have connected the Platform; data as per Section 5.
8.4. The processor acts only on documented instructions, ensures confidentiality, implements
security measures (Art. 32 GDPR), engages sub-processors only with consent, assists in responding to data subject
requests, notifies of incidents, deletes or returns the data upon completion, and provides information for
audits.
8.5. Transfers to third countries only where there is a lawful basis (an adequacy decision, standard
contractual clauses or other).

## 9. Term and Termination

This Agreement is concluded for an indefinite term. Either party may terminate it by giving the other party 30 days' notice. After
termination, My EPA stops receiving data and deletes the data received from the Platform within 30 days,
informing students that the Platform has been disconnected.
