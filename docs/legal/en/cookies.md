# My EPA Cookie Policy

The Service uses only what is necessary for it to work: one cookie and the browser's local storage.
There are no advertising or analytics cookies, so no separate consent to cookies is required.

| What | Where | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `refreshToken` | cookie (httpOnly, secure) | staying signed in without logging in again | up to 7 days |
| `accessToken`, `refreshToken`, `currentUser` | browser local storage | operation of the personal account after login | until logout |
| `myepa-lang` | browser local storage | the selected website language | until cleared |

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings; after that, you will need to log in again and
select the language again.

If the Service starts using analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will update this page and
ask for consent before setting them.

Questions: support@myepa.ru.
