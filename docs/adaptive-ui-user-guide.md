# Adaptive UI Color Scheme User Guide

## What is the Adaptive UI Feature?

The Adaptive UI color scheme is a unique feature of our education platform that allows the application interface to adapt based on:

1. Your current mood
2. The time of day
3. Your personal preferences

## Why Use Adaptive UI?

Research has shown that color can influence cognitive function, focus, and emotional state. The Adaptive UI feature aims to:

- Enhance your learning experience by matching the interface color to your current needs
- Reduce eye strain during different times of day
- Create a more personalized and engaging environment

## Available Mood Themes

### Neutral (Blue)
- Default theme with a balanced blue color scheme
- Best for: General use, neutral emotional states
- Color: `hsl(210, 100%, 50%)`

### Calm (Teal)
- Soothing teal/blue color tones designed to promote relaxation
- Best for: Evening study, stress reduction, content review
- Color: `hsl(190, 90%, 50%)`

### Focused (Purple)
- Deep purple tones designed to enhance concentration
- Best for: Deep work sessions, complex problem-solving, exams
- Color: `hsl(263, 70%, 50%)`

### Energetic (Orange)
- Vibrant orange that promotes creativity and energy
- Best for: Brainstorming, morning sessions, creative tasks
- Color: `hsl(33, 100%, 50%)`

## Time-Based Automatic Suggestions

The system will automatically suggest a mood theme based on the time of day:

| Time of Day | Suggested Mood | Rationale |
|------------|---------------|-----------|
| Morning (5am-9am) | Energetic | Helps wake up your brain and get energized for the day |
| Daytime (9am-5pm) | Focused | Supports concentration during primary work/study hours |
| Evening (5pm-10pm) | Calm | Helps wind down and reduce blue light exposure |
| Night (10pm-5am) | Neutral | Balanced option for late night or early morning sessions |

## How to Change Your Mood Theme

1. Navigate to **Settings** from the main navigation menu
2. Select the **Appearance** tab
3. Scroll to the **Mood Theme** section
4. Choose your preferred mood option
5. Click **Save Preferences** to apply changes

## Customization Tips

- **Consistent Focus**: Use the Focused (purple) theme when preparing for exams or during intensive study periods
- **Evening Reading**: Switch to Calm (teal) when reading course materials in the evening
- **Morning Boost**: Start your day with the Energetic (orange) theme to boost motivation
- **Default Mode**: When unsure, the Neutral (blue) theme provides a balanced experience

## Technical Details

- Your mood theme preference is saved both locally and to your account
- The system will remember your preference across different devices
- Time-based suggestions only apply if you haven't manually selected a mood theme
